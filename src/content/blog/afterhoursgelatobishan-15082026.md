After Hours Gelato needs no introduction to late-night dessert lovers. Known for their gelato and churros mochi waffles, they recently opened their second outlet in Bishan, right at 150A Bishan Street 11. The space spans two storeys in a modern silver-and-orange theme, and with plenty of seating it's easy to find a spot. Staying open till 2am daily, it's the kind of place you can wander into after dinner and leave fully satisfied.

We ordered two waffles, each topped with two scoops of gelato. The first was Dark Chocolate and Brownies n' Cream, and the second was Korean Strawberry and Vanilla.

The ice cream was lovely — smooth and creamy, with a texture that was just right. Both waffles were served with nicely sized scoops, and the gelato sat beautifully on the warm waffles. Of the four flavours, Brownies n' Cream was the standout and easily my favourite, with rich brownie chunks folded into creamy ice cream. The Dark Chocolate was solid and not too sweet, and the Vanilla was clean and classic. The Korean Strawberry was the only letdown — it tasted a little bit artificial, like the flavour of strawberry syrup more than real fruit.

The waffles themselves were great. They were nice and crispy on the outside, yet fluffy on the inside, holding up well against the melting gelato without turning soggy.

Overall, a very nice experience. The gelato was creamy, the waffles were well executed, and the late-night hours make it a handy spot for a dessert fix. I'd definitely come back for the Brownies n' Cream.
